01 · Why it exists

Mission

Mission

Real-world research for the entheogen and psychedelic wellness movement, from members who keep a real practice.

Ethos: help people keep a real practice, and show whether it's changing them.

The problem

Consistency is the hard part of any self-work, and no one can yet show what actually helps.

The answer

Make showing up rewarding, then measure before and after, so members and the movement can see the real effect.

04 · The research

What it measures, and why it's different

Equilibrium follows each member for months to see which mix of daily practices, with or without plant medicines, goes with real change. Because it counts the people who don't respond too, it can show what works for whom, not just on average.

Every evening

Mood, energy, stress, focus, sleep. Dosed, practiced, worked on a goal.

Every week

Validated scales (WHO-5, PHQ-2, GAD-2; PSS-4 monthly), side effects with severity, a safety question.

Dose and practice days

Micro, macro or none, compared with each person's own off days and starting point.

Real-life function

Goals, steps and wins, as weekly counts.

Different because

Months, not weeks

Each person is followed from their starting point for a year and more.

Each person vs their own start

Change is measured against the member's own baseline.

Many practices, not one medicine

Breath, stillness, movement, goals, with or without a dose.

Everyone counts

Non-dosers and non-responders are part of the picture.

Structured experience report

1

Before

Intention, set, setting, mood.

2

The dose

Offering, amount and time, from the dose log.

3

The experience

Timeline, effects checklist, the member's own story.

4

After

Next-day check-in and integration questions.

5

Follow-ups

One week, one month, three months: what changed?

Every step is structured data. The story itself stays private to the member. Findings are reported as patterns, not proof, until an independent ethics review.

06 · Structure

Three layers on a science base

Layer 3 · The Academy level I free · II+ membership

Schools as classes: Contemplative, Hermetic, Depth psychology, Body and breath. Masters, levels I–IV, trials. Max two at a time, like an RPG job system.

Layer 2 · The Path free

Universal main rank: Foundation → Acolyte → Initiate → Adept. Short lessons. Rank rises only by invitation after real practice.

Layer 1 · The daily loop free

Guided session, top 3 goals, dose log (if opted in), evening check-in, journal, wins. A few minutes a day.

Underneath · Citizen science free · a clear yes

Check-ins feed a de-identified research copy only after a clear yes. Turning it off takes two steps, any time. Every feature works without sharing.

A new member only has to understand the bottom layer. The higher layers reveal themselves as they climb. Simple mode shows the daily loop, the Path and Contemplative I practices; Advanced mode opens the map and the Academy.

07 · Two modes + app map

Simple first. Advanced when you're ready.

Everyone starts in Simple: one calm Today screen, no long scrolls. Advanced is free and opt in; it opens the world map and the Academy around the same daily loop.

Simpledefault

  • TodayOpens here. One Begin card (the next Path lesson), then goals, dose log (if opted in) and evening check-in.
  • ProgressPath rank and lessons, goals, wins, then vs now, Light and gems, garden. "Go deeper" at the bottom: the Hall of Spirit and Advanced mode.
  • JournalReflections, check-in notes, experience reports.

Advancedopt in, free

  • WorldOpens here. Academy map with the Path, school buildings, the garden and the Hall of Spirit. A collapsible Today tray on top.
  • CharacterRank bar, element constellations, classes, wins, goals, bag, gems, settings.
  • JournalEverything in Simple, plus Compass and the soul mirror once unlocked.

The Path lives in both

Its next lesson fills Today's Begin card; its rank and lessons sit in Progress (Character in Advanced).

Breath and stillness from day one

Simple's "Other practices" are Contemplative Level I: mindful breathing, listening, body scan, ten quiet minutes, the mindful walk, three thanks.

Practice carries in

Shelf practice is saved now. If you later join Contemplative, it counts toward Level I. Joining stays a choice, by invitation.

Hall of Spirit · Be Well Ministry

A wing of the academy for adult members, free to join with an optional donation. Joining is read, screened and agreed to: the covenant, a Safety Screening and informed consent.

One journey

Everything you record lives in one journey, so nothing gets split or lost, and data is recorded the same way for everyone.

Support on every screen

A Support button floats on every screen in both modes, with psychedelic peer support first.

11 · Rewards

Two currencies: honor and proof

✦ Light (XP)never redeemable

  • Honor systemEvery session (+20), a full-sentence line (+10), notes, finished steps and goals.
  • What it growsLights stars, grows the garden, fills rank. Faking it only fools yourself.

◆ Gemsredeemable later

  • Proof-backed1 per proven day (a timed session + the evening check-in) and 1 per full line.
  • A steady week+7 when 5 of 7 days are proven.

Your journal is private to you. Anything you share for research has no name, email or account attached. Your research data goes only to research: universities, health organizations and research partners, under agreements that forbid trying to identify anyone. It is never sold or licensed to advertisers, insurers, employers, data brokers or law enforcement, and we publish who receives it. Draft wording, under legal review.

Groups of 10 or more

Every shared number covers at least 10 people. Smaller groups say "not enough yet."

Readings only

Research copies keep readings and age ranges: no notes, no journal text, no exact dates, no names.

Research licenses only

Row-level data only under a signed data-use agreement with the partner's ethics approval: no re-identifying, no resale.

No ads, ever

No advertising or analytics code in the app.

Under 18 stays on the device

Reflection mode, no sign-in, never in research.

Not medical care

No one reviews answers in real time. Support is on every screen.

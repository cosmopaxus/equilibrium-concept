04 · Why now

Programs are moving. Measurement isn't.

States are opening access faster than they're building ways to measure it. Equilibrium brings long-term, de-identified data to that gap, at no cost to the state.

Federal signal · EO 14401$50M+

For HHS to partner with states, share data and expand real-world evidence on psychedelics.

FDA · Sep 2026Data on the agenda

Its public hearing named data collection and standardization as a topic.

  • Oregon: state reports cover the session and the 3 days after it
  • Colorado: a de-identified outcome database is allowed, but only if gifts or grants pay for it
  • Microdosing: no state program follows it, or daily practice, over months

What works, for whom, over time?

01 · The question

Trials ask if something works on average.
People need to know what works for them.

What the field measures

  • The averageOne effect across a whole group
  • Weeks to a few monthsThen follow-up ends
  • One medicine at a timePractice and daily life left out
  • The clinicSession days, not ordinary days

What Equilibrium measures

  • Each person against their own startChange for this person, not just the group
  • Months and yearsA short check-in every evening, scales every week
  • Many practices, with or without a doseBreath, stillness, movement, goals; micro, macro or none
  • Everyone countsPeople who don't respond, and people who never dose

Each person is their own control

Compare a member's weeks with one mix against weeks with another.

2

Test a short list first

Practitioners name 3–5 likely mixes before the data comes in.

3

Follow the non-responders

No change by week 12? See which dial they add before change shows.

In the app now: practice days and dose days, each person against their own start. Next: the eight practice dials on the evening check-in, filled in automatically from what members do in the app.

03 · Why now

Programs are moving. Measurement isn't.

States are opening access faster than they're building ways to measure it. Equilibrium brings long-term, de-identified data to that gap, at no cost to the state.

Federal signal · Apr 2026EO 14401

Directs HHS to partner with states and expand real-world evidence on psychedelics.

FDA · Sep 2026Data, on the agenda

Its public hearing named data collection and standardization as a topic.

  • Oregon: state reports cover the session and the 3 days after it
  • Colorado: a de-identified outcome database is allowed, but only if gifts or grants pay for it
  • Microdosing: no state program follows it, or daily practice, over months

Patterns, not proof. No member data is studied before an independent ethics review.

Live today

  • Guided practice, with levels from 3 to 10 minutes
  • Evening and weekly check-ins
  • Goals, steps and wins
  • An optional, private dose log
  • Be Well Ministry joining: covenant, Safety Screening, consent
  • Sign-in and sync across devices
  • 900+ automated checks before every release

Trust is built in

  • Research sharing only after a clear yes
  • De-identified: no names, notes or journal text
  • Shown only in groups of 10 or more
  • Research partners only, never advertisers or data brokers
  • No ads, no trackers

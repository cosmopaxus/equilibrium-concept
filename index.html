01 · The question

Trials ask if something works on average. People need to know what works for them.

What the field measures

  • The averageOne effect across a whole group
  • Weeks to a few monthsThen follow-up ends
  • One medicine at a timePractice and daily life left out
  • The clinicSession days, not ordinary days

What Equilibrium measures

  • Each person against their own startChange for this person, not just the group
  • Months and yearsA short check-in every evening, scales every week
  • Many practices, with or without a doseBreath, stillness, movement, goals; micro, macro or none
  • Everyone countsPeople who don't respond, and people who never dose

02 · The combination lock

For some people, no single thing works. A set of dials has to line up.

An idea from New Mexico's psychedelic community. Equilibrium records which dials were turned each day, then looks for the mix that comes before change.

Why you can't just test every mix

Every full mix · 768 groups · 7,680 people
Pairs of practices · 84 groups · 840 people
Each person vs themselves · works from one person

8 practices × 3 dose states, at least 10 people behind every number.

How Equilibrium cracks it

1

Own control

Compare each member's weeks with one mix against weeks with another

2

A short list first

Practitioners name 3–5 mixes before the data comes in

3

Follow non-responders

No change by week 12? See which dial they add before change shows

✓

A map

What works for whom, as patterns, not proof

In the app now, and next

Now: practice days and dose days, each person against their own start. Next: the eight practice dials on the evening check-in, filled in automatically from what members do in the app.

03 · Why now

States are opening access faster than they're building ways to measure it.

Federal push Apr 2026

Executive Order 14401 directs HHS to partner with states and expand real-world evidence on psychedelics.

FDA asked about data Sep 2026

Its public hearing put data collection and standardization on the agenda.

New Mexico

Its new psilocybin program is writing its rules now, and hasn't set what it will measure.

Oregon

State reports cover the session and the 3 days after it.

Colorado

A law allows a de-identified outcome database, but only if gifts or grants pay for it.

Microdosing

No state program follows microdosing or daily practice over months.

Equilibrium brings long-term, de-identified data to that gap, at no cost to the state.

04 · The working app

Not a concept. Release 4.5.1 is live with a small test group.

How a day becomes evidence

1

Practice

A few timed minutes off screen: breath, stillness, a walk, a lesson

2

Evening check-in

Mood, energy, stress, focus, sleep, pressure, resource · 30 seconds

3

Weekly scales

Validated measures: WHO-5, PHQ-2, GAD-2, side effects

✓

Then vs now

Each person against their own start, from day 14

Live today4.5.1

  • Guided practice, with levels from 3 to 10 minutes
  • Evening and weekly check-ins
  • Goals, steps and wins
  • An optional, private dose log
  • Be Well Ministry joining: covenant, Safety Screening, consent
  • Sign-in and sync across devices
  • 900+ automated checks before every release

Trust is built in

  • Research sharing only after a clear yes
  • De-identified: no names, notes or journal text
  • Shown only in groups of 10 or more
  • Research partners only, never advertisers or data brokers
  • No ads, no trackers
  • An independent ethics review before any data is studied

How it stays free

  • Free coreThe daily loop, the path and research, for every member
  • Academy membershipDeeper levels in each school of practice
  • Research licensesDe-identified data for research and health partners

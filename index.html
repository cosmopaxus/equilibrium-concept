01 · Why it exists

Mission

Mission

Real-world research for the entheogen and psychedelic wellness movement, from members who keep a real practice.

Ethos: help people keep a real practice, and show whether it's changing them.
?
The problemConsistency is the hard part of any self-work, and no one can yet show what actually helps.
✓

04 · The research

What it measures, and why it's different

Equilibrium follows each member for months to see which mix of daily practices, with or without plant medicines, goes with real change. Because it counts the people who don't respond too, it can show what works for whom, not just on average.

☾
Every eveningMood, energy, stress, focus, sleep. Dosed, practiced, worked on a goal.
▦
Every weekValidated scales (WHO-5, PHQ-2, GAD-2; PSS-4 monthly), side effects with severity, a safety question.
◐
Dose and practice daysMicro, macro or none, compared with each person's own off days and starting point.
✓
Real-life functionGoals, steps and wins, as weekly counts.

Different because

⌛
Months, not weeksEach person is followed from their starting point for a year and more.
⇄
Each person vs their own startChange is measured against the member's own baseline.
✳
Many practices, not one medicineBreath, stillness, movement, goals, with or without a dose.
≠
Everyone countsNon-dosers and non-responders are part of the picture.

Structured experience report

1 · BeforeIntention, set, setting, mood
→
2 · The doseOffering, amount and time, from the dose log
→
3 · The experienceTimeline, effects checklist, the member's own story
→
4 · AfterNext-day check-in and integration questions
→
5 · Follow-upsOne week, one month, three months: what changed?

Every step is structured data. The story itself stays private to the member. Findings are reported as patterns, not proof, until an independent ethics review.

06 · Structure

Three layers on a science base

♜
Layer 3 · The Academy level I free · II+ membershipSchools as classes: Contemplative, Hermetic, Depth psychology, Body and breath. Masters, levels I–IV, trials. Max two at a time, like an RPG job system.
⟿
Layer 2 · The Path freeUniversal main rank: Foundation → Acolyte → Initiate → Adept. Short lessons. Rank rises only by invitation after real practice.
☼
Layer 1 · The daily loop freeGuided session, top 3 goals, dose log (if opted in), evening check-in, journal, wins. A few minutes a day.
⚗
Underneath · Citizen science free · a clear yesCheck-ins feed a de-identified research copy only after a clear yes. Turning it off takes two steps, any time. Every feature works without sharing.

A new member only has to understand the bottom layer. The higher layers reveal themselves as they climb. Simple mode shows the daily loop, the Path and Contemplative I practices; Advanced mode opens the map and the Academy.
